Physical Characteristics
Tigers have orange fur with black stripes, unique to each individual. They have powerful muscles, strong jaws, and sharp claws for hunting. Tigers are the largest cat species in the world.

Habitat & Distribution
Found in Asia, mainly in India, Russia, China, and Southeast Asia. Prefer forests, grasslands, and mangrove swamps.

Diet & Hunting
Tigers are carnivores and hunt deer, wild boar, and buffalo. They are solitary hunters, using stealth and strength to catch prey. Can leap up to 10 meters (33 feet) to catch prey.

Behavior & Social Structure
Mostly solitary, except during mating or when a mother raises cubs. They mark their territory using scent marks and claw marks on trees. Can swim well and often cool off in water.

Reproduction
Female tigers give birth to 2-4 cubs after a 3.5-month pregnancy. Cubs stay with their mother for about 2 years before becoming independent.

Conservation Status
Tigers are endangered due to poaching, habitat loss, and human-wildlife conflict. Global tiger population is estimated at less than 4,000 in the wild.

WHY ARE TIGERS UNDER THREAT?

PPOACHING
Tiger parts have been used in traditional Asian medicine for hundreds of years to treat things like rheumatism, leprosy, cataracts, toothache, various skin diseases, muscle aches and malaria.

CLIMATE CHANGE
Climate change could also speed up this habitat loss. If the projected sea level rise happens in the watery Sunderbans swamps, it would submerge 96% of the tiger’s breeding habitat there.

HUMAN AND WILDLIFE CONFLICT
Contact between tigers and people is on the increase, and that’s down to an ever-growing human population. The number of people living in Asia’s 13 different tiger range countries doubled in 40 years to 3.2bn in 2005.

Ways to Save Tigers from Extinction

Protect Tiger Habitats
Support national parks and wildlife sanctuaries. Reduce human activities like mining and agriculture in tiger habitats.

Stop Poaching & Illegal Trade
Stronger laws and enforcement against tiger hunting. Ban the trade of tiger skins, bones, and body parts.Support anti-poaching patrols and wildlife crime investigations.

Increase Awareness & Education
Educate people about the importance of tigers in the ecosystem. Support World Tiger Day (July 29) and other conservation events.
